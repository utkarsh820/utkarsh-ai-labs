# Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)

Welcome to the Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) module! This section comprehensively covers the foundations of computer vision, architectural paradigms, and modern state-of-the-art techniques.

Below are the detailed topics covered in this module:

1. [Introduction to CNNs](01-What-Are-CNNs.md): Understand the basics, spatial awareness, and mathematical foundations of convolutions.
2. [CNN Architecture & Essential Structures](02-CNN-Architecture.md): Deep dive into layers (Conv, Pool, FC) and famous architectures (ResNet, VGG, Inception).
3. [Transfer Learning](03-Transfer-Learning.md): Learn how to leverage pre-trained models on new, smaller datasets with freezing and fine-tuning strategies.
4. [Vision Transformers (ViT)](04-Vision-Transformers.md): Explore the paradigm shift from Convolutions to Self-Attention in image processing.
5. [Stable Diffusion & Generative AI](05-Stable-Diffusion.md): Understand Latent Diffusion, U-Nets, and how AI generates breathtaking imagery from text alone.
