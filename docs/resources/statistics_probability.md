---

## 📘 How to Use This Learning Path

**The Strategy:**

1.  **Start:** Pick **one** resource from "Beginner" to grasp intuition without complex math notation.
2.  **Practice:** Calculate mean, variance, and standard deviation manually, then in Python.
3.  **Progress:** Move to "Intermediate" to understand distributions and hypothesis testing.
4.  **Master:** Use "Elite" resources to explore Bayesian statistics and mathematical derivations.

## 💡 Pro Tips for Success

1.  **Code the Math:** Don't just read formulas. Generate 1000 random variables in Python and plot their distribution.
2.  **Beware of p-values:** Understand that `p < 0.05` does not mean your hypothesis is true. It means the data is unlikely given the null hypothesis.
3.  **Draw the Distributions:** Whenever working with statistical problems, draw a picture of the distribution and label the means/tails.
