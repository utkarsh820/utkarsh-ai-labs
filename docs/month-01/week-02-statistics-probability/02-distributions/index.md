# Probability Distributions

Welcome to the Distributions module of the Academy. In this section, we explore the mathematical models that describe the "shape" of data in the real world. By understanding probability distributions, you can predict the likelihood of different outcomes, model uncertainty, and make data-driven decisions.

## Curriculum Structure

This module is designed following our 5-level format (Intuition, Definition, Formula, Examples, and Case Study) to ensure a deep, practical understanding:

1. [Introduction to Distributions](./01-Introduction-to-Distributions.md)
   - Understand the core concept of how data spreads out and what Probability Density Functions (PDFs) and Probability Mass Functions (PMFs mean.

2. [Discrete Distributions](./02-Discrete-Distributions.md)
   - Explore distributions dealing with countable outcomes, specifically the **Binomial** and **Poisson** distributions.

3. [Continuous Distributions](./03-Continuous-Distributions.md)
   - Dive into distributions with continuous outcomes, focusing on the foundational **Normal** (Gaussian) and **Uniform** distributions.

4. [Central Limit Theorem](./04-Central-Limit-Theorem.md)
   - Uncover one of the most important concepts in all of statistics—how sample averages magically form a normal curve, empowering everything from A/B testing to Machine Learning.

By the end of this module, you will have a rock-solid foundation in modeling statistical data distributions with Python.
